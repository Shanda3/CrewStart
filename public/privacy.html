Privacy note

Privacy

Updated November 20, 2026. Your answers to the questions stay in the browser, except when you use the optional Smart AI suggestions. Nothing is saved to our email list unless you join it. Crewstart has no accounts. We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and doesn't collect personal data or track you across sites. Joining the list is optional, and your prompt never depends on it.

  • Email is optional

    You can copy your prompt without it. If you join the list, your email is used only for the list, and only if you tick that box. If you use Smart AI suggestions, your email is used to limit runs (3 per day) and is stored only as a salted hash, unless you also opt into the list.
  • What's stored

    The list is stored in Cloudflare D1. If you join, we save your email, your first name if you add it, your two checkbox choices, the date and time, and the source (Crewstart). Your role and industry are saved only if you tick that box. Smart AI rate-limit counters store only a salted email hash and a daily total, and are deleted after about 2 days.
  • Who sees it

    Only Shanda Biggs.
  • No selling

    Your details are never sold, rented, or shared for ads.
  • Spam protection

    Cloudflare Turnstile checks that you're a person when you submit.
  • Smart AI suggestions

    This add-on is optional. If you use it, Crewstart sends your answers (profession, location, tasks, tools, and goals — not your email or name) to OpenAI to generate suggestions. Your email is used only for rate limiting as a salted hash unless you opt into the email list. The counters are deleted after about 2 days.
  • Unsubscribe or delete

    Every email has a one-click unsubscribe. To delete your details, reply to any email from Shanda or contact [contact email to come].
