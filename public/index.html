Crewstart: a team builder for your Grok Bot

Answer a few quick questions about your work and life. You get a ready-to-paste setup prompt for your first bot, plus a plain-words checklist of what each connection allows.

  • About 5 minutes. Five short steps, sensible defaults.
  • No sign-in. Your answers and your prompt stay in this browser tab. Email is optional, and we'll only email you if you join the list and say so.
  • Safe by default. Your bots always ask before sending messages or spending money. This tool never asks for passwords or keys.
  • You stay in control. This tool doesn't connect anything. You approve each connection yourself, inside your bot.

            What happens next
            
          
              
            
            1. Open your first bot and paste the prompt above.
              2. 
            
            2. Your bot offers to add the suggested templates and creates each custom bot with the name and job listed.
              3. 
            
            3. For each connection, your bot checks whether it exists and asks you to approve it. Say no to anything you don't want; it gets skipped.
              4. 
            
            4. Your bot sets up the routines (briefings and digests) at the times you chose.
              5. 
            
            5. You get a summary: templates added, custom bots created, connections made, anything skipped. Change anything later by asking your bot.
